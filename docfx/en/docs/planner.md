# Planner

With Planner, you can see your Microsoft Outlook events right inside Slingshot, alongside the work you're planning. Join online meetings with one click, review meeting details and attendees, and turn Teams meeting transcripts into summaries and action items.

Planner is personal. The events you see belong to your connected account, not to a workspace, so other members never see your calendar.

> [!NOTE]
> Some Planner capabilities, such as meeting recaps, are being rolled out gradually and may not be available on every account yet.

## Opening Planner

Click/tap on **Planner** in the side rail. Planner opens in **Week** view, scrolled to just before the current time.

### Changing the View

Use the view options on the toolbar to switch between:

| View | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Day** | A single day, hour by hour. |
| **Week** | Seven days, hour by hour. This is the default. |
| **Work Week** | Monday to Friday, with weekends hidden. |
| **Month** | The whole month. When a day has more events than fit, click/tap on **+N more** to see them all. |

Slingshot remembers the view you pick, so it's the same the next time you open Planner, on any device. Click/tap on **Today** to jump back to the current time.

## Connecting Your Outlook Calendar

1. On the Planner toolbar, click/tap on **Add Calendar**.
2. Sign in to your Microsoft account.
3. Grant Slingshot access to your calendar.

Your Outlook events appear on the grid right away.

> [!NOTE]
> Slingshot only asks for calendar permissions when you connect. If you later use a feature that needs more access, such as meeting recaps, Slingshot asks for that permission at that time.

### Choosing Which Calendars to Show

Click/tap on **My Calendars** on the toolbar to see your connected calendars. Check or uncheck calendars to show or hide their events. By default, only your primary calendar is shown.

Your selection is saved and stays in sync across your devices.

### Keeping Your Calendar Up to Date

Changes made in Outlook, including changes made by other people, show up in Planner automatically. To force a full refresh, click/tap on **Refresh** on the toolbar.

> [!NOTE]
> A newly added calendar can take up to 15 minutes to appear in the **My Calendars** list.

### If Your Sign-In Expires

If your Outlook sign-in expires, Slingshot shows a message above the calendar: "Your Outlook sign-in expired." Click/tap on **Reauthenticate** to sign in again. Until you do, you can still see the events you already loaded, but they may not be up to date.

## Reading Your Calendar

Events take the color of the calendar they belong to, matching the colors you set in Outlook.

- **All-day events** appear in the band at the top of each day. All-day events marked as busy or out of office shade the whole day.
- **Recurring events** show a repeat icon.
- **Past events** appear faded, and **cancelled events** are faded even more.
- The colored stripe on the edge of each event reflects how you appear: busy, free, or out of office.

Hover over an event to see its title and time.

### Joining Online Meetings

Online meetings, such as Microsoft Teams meetings, show a **Join** button on the event in **Day**, **Week**, and **Work Week** views. The button appears 15 minutes before the meeting starts and stays until it ends. Click/tap on **Join** to open the meeting.

### Viewing Event Details

- **Click/tap on an event** to open a quick preview with the date and time, location, organizer, attendee responses, attachments, and a **Join Meeting** link when there is one.
- **Double-click/tap an event**, or click/tap on the expand button in the preview, to open the full details panel.

The details panel shows the full description and the attendee list, grouped into **Organizer**, **Accepted**, **Tentative**, **Did not respond**, and **Declined**. From the top of the panel you can:

- Move to the **previous** or **next** event.
- **Copy a link** to the event to share in a Slingshot chat.
- **Open the event in Outlook** on the web.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Slingshot Tip**: Keep the details panel open and click/tap on other events in the calendar. The panel updates to show each one, so you can review your day quickly.

You can also right-click an event to **Copy link** or **Open in new window**.

## Meeting Recaps and Action Items

For finished Microsoft Teams meetings, the details panel includes a **Recap** tab that uses the meeting transcript to help you follow up.

> [!NOTE]
> Recaps need access to your Teams transcripts. If Slingshot doesn't have it yet, click/tap on **Request Access**. Transcripts can take up to a couple of hours to become available after the meeting ends.

### Summaries

- Click/tap on **View Recap** to open the Teams recording.
- Click/tap on **Generate Summary** to create an AI summary of the meeting. You can copy the summary to your clipboard, and the organizer can update it.

### Action Items

The **Action Items** list collects tasks linked to the meeting.

- Click/tap on **+ Task** to add your own task. Use the location control at the end of the row to choose which task list it goes to.
- Click/tap on **Create from Transcript** to have Slingshot suggest action items from the transcript, each with a suggested owner.

For each suggestion, click/tap **✓** to keep it as a task or **✕** to dismiss it. You can also use **Keep All** or **Dismiss All**. Click/tap a suggestion to edit it before keeping it.

Action items are shared with everyone in your organization who attended the meeting, although each person only sees tasks they have access to. For recurring meetings, the same action items appear on every occurrence.

## Tips

- **Pick your view once**: Slingshot remembers your view and calendar selection everywhere you sign in.
- **Use Work Week** to focus on business days.
- **Add the Upcoming Events widget** to a personal Overview to see the next 30 days of events at a glance.
