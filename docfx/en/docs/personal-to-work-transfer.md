# Moving Your Work from a Personal Account to Your Work Account

If you started using Slingshot with a personal email and your company now uses Slingshot with your work email, you don't have to start over. With **Move to my work account**, you can quickly copy your workspaces and personal task lists from your personal account into your work account, with your tasks, dates, and task types intact.

A transfer is a copy. Everything in your personal account stays exactly as it was.

> [!NOTE]
> You need access to both accounts. You send the transfer from your personal account, and you accept it while signed in to your work account.

## What You Can Move

| Item | Who Can Move It |
|---|---|
| **Workspaces** | The **workspace owner**, from a personal or trial account |
| **Personal task lists** (lists in your own **My Tasks**, including **Personal Tasks**) | You, from a personal or trial account |

**Move to my work account** isn't available when:

- The workspace belongs to a company account.
- Your account is a work account, or you are a member of a company account.
- The workspace is archived.
- The task list belongs to a workspace or is shared with you. Lists inside a workspace move with their workspace.

> [!NOTE]
> Trial accounts count as personal accounts, even after the trial has ended.

## What Gets Copied

| Copied | Not Copied |
|---|---|
| Workspaces, projects, task lists, and sections | Comments and activity |
| Tasks with their titles, descriptions, statuses, and field values | Attachments |
| Original start and due dates | Time logs |
| Task types and custom fields | Automations |
| Dashboards and overviews | Workspace members |
| Task dependencies and subtasks | @mentions |

Tasks assigned to people in your personal account are reassigned when the transfer is accepted (see [Step 3: Assign People](#step-3-assign-people)).

## How to Send a Transfer

### Step 1: Start the Transfer

You can start a transfer in two ways:

- **From a workspace**: Click/tap the workspace's overflow menu and choose **Move to my work account**. The workspace settings open on the **Transfers** tab.
- **From a personal task list**: Click/tap the list's overflow menu in **My Tasks** and choose **Move to my work account**. Your account settings open on the **Transfers** page.

The **Move to your work account** sheet opens on top, with the item you started from already selected.

### Step 2: Choose What to Move

The sheet lists everything you can move, in two groups:

- **Workspaces**: the workspaces you own in your personal account.
- **My task lists**: the lists in your own **My Tasks**.

Select every item you want to move. You can send workspaces and lists together in a single transfer.

### Step 3: Enter Your Work Email and Send

Enter the email address of your work account and click/tap **Send link**.

Slingshot emails a link to that address. The link expires in **7 days** and can only be accepted by someone signed in with that exact email address.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Slingshot Tip**: Double-check the address before sending. A transfer sent to a mistyped address can't be accepted. If that happens, cancel it and send a new one.

You can't send a transfer to the same email address as the account you're sending from.

## How to Track Your Transfers

Every transfer you send appears as a card:

- In the workspace's settings, on the **Transfers** tab.
- In your account settings, on the **Transfers** page, which lists everything you've sent.

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting for acceptance** | The link has been sent and not yet accepted. | **Resend link** or **Cancel transfer** |
| **Accepted by** _name_ | Your work account accepted the transfer. | Nothing further. The card is a record. |
| **Expired** | The link wasn't accepted within 7 days. | **Send again** |

- **Resend link** sends the same email again. It doesn't extend the expiry. You can resend a link once a minute, and send up to 10 transfer emails per hour.
- **Cancel transfer** asks you to confirm, then stops the link from working.
- **Move to my work account** at the bottom of the page starts a new transfer at any time.

## How to Accept a Transfer

### Step 1: Open the Link

Open the email on any device and click/tap the link. Sign in with your **work** email address, or create your work account if you don't have one yet.

The **Accept transfer** page shows who sent the transfer and what it contains.

### Step 2: Review the Transfer

The page lists each item in the transfer and where it will go:

- **Workspaces** are added to your company's account.
- **Personal task lists** are added to your own **My Tasks**. They are new lists, never merged into lists you already have.

If the transfer includes your **Personal Tasks** list, it arrives as a new list named "Personal Tasks (from _sender name_)" so it doesn't mix with your own.

Task types and custom fields used by your personal task lists are added to your personal library, so you can use them on any of your personal lists.

### Step 3: Assign People

If any tasks are assigned to people, the **Assign people** card lists each person. Tasks assigned to you in your personal account are automatically reassigned to you.

For everyone else, choose one of:

- **A member of your company**: Slingshot suggests a match by email address, then by name. Click/tap the dropdown to search for someone else.
- **Invite** _email_: the tasks are assigned to that address, and the person receives an invitation.
- **Leave unassigned**: the tasks arrive with no assignee.

### Step 4: Accept the Transfer

Click/tap **Accept transfer**. When the copy is ready, Slingshot opens the new workspace, or your **My Tasks** if you moved only task lists.

The sender gets an email letting them know the transfer was accepted.

> [!NOTE]
> If some items can't be copied, the page stays open. The items that were copied are removed from the list, and you can click/tap **Accept transfer** again to retry the rest.

Click/tap **Not now** to leave without accepting. The link keeps working until it expires.

## If Something Goes Wrong

| Message | What It Means |
|---|---|
| This transfer was sent to a different email address. | You're signed in with an account other than the one the link was sent to. Sign in with the email address that received the link. |
| Sign in with your work account to accept this transfer. | Transfers can only be accepted into a work account, not another personal account. |
| You belong to more than one work account. | Slingshot can't yet choose which company to add the transfer to. |
| Invitation expired | The link has expired, was canceled, or was already accepted. Ask the sender to send a new transfer. |
| This workspace belongs to a company account and can't be moved. | Only workspaces in a personal or trial account can be moved. |
| You've sent this recently. | Wait a minute before resending, or try again later if you've sent many transfers in the last hour. |

## FAQ

**Does moving delete anything from my personal account?**
No. A transfer is a copy. Your personal account isn't changed.

**Will the people in my personal workspace be added to the copy?**
No. Workspace members aren't copied. Only you are a member of the new workspace, and you can invite people as usual.

**Can I move the same workspace more than once?**
Yes. Each accepted transfer creates a new copy.

**What if I edit my workspace after sending?**
The transfer contains the content as it was when you sent it. To include later changes, cancel the transfer and send a new one.
