# YouTube

The YouTube data source allows you to connect your *YouTube channel* data to Slingshot. Use your channel analytics to create insightful dashboards and measure your video content performance, audience engagement, and channel growth.

## Prerequisites

Before you try using the *YouTube* data source in Analytics, make sure that:

- You have a <a href="https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1646861" target="_blank">YouTube channel</a> associated with your Google account.

- You have access to <a href="https://studio.youtube.com" target="_blank">YouTube Studio</a> for the channel you want to connect.

## Connecting a YouTube Account to Slingshot

To add a *YouTube* data source to your list, follow the steps described below:

1. Find **My Analytics** section, go to **Dashboards** and click **+Create Dashboard**.

2. Click on the **+Data Source** button.

3. Select **YouTube Analytics** as your data source. You can find it under **Social Media** in the data sources list.

4. You will see Google's login screen. Enter your login credentials and click *Sign In*.

5. An authorization prompt will appear. Click *Allow* to grant Slingshot access to your YouTube data.

6. If you have multiple YouTube channels associated with your account, select the channel you want to analyze.

7. In the dialog that opens, you can change the data source name, add a description, see if the data source is [certified](../../../certifications.md) (available to *Enterprise* users), and edit the details. Click **Add Data Source** to finish the process.

> [!NOTE]
> Once logged in, you can use the same account later to create another visualization unless you delete the data source.

## Setting Up Your Data

From the *Data Sources* list, select the YouTube account you want to connect. You will see the *Data Source details* dialog, which allows you to review and set up your data.

Here you will find the following data source details:

- Type and Name

- Description

- [Certification](../../../certifications.md)

- Who added the data source and when

- Who last modified it and when

- Who (users and workspaces) has access to it

- How often the data is refreshed. To change the time period, select the drop-down menu on the right.

When ready, click **Select Data** to continue to the *Visualization Editor*.

## Working in the Visualization Editor

Once your data source has been added, you will be taken to the *Visualization Editor*.

You will be presented with two sections in their own query field:

- **Dimensions** (depicted by a cube icon with a pink side): Dimensions are attributes of your data. For example, the dimension *Video Title* represents the title of a video on your channel.

- **Measures** (depicted by *123* icon): Measures consist of numeric data. For example, you can track *Views*, *Watch Time*, *Likes*, *Comments*, and *Subscribers Gained* for your channel.

By default, the *Column* visualization will be selected. Click on it to choose another chart type from the drop-down menu.

## The Date Range Data Filter

You can filter your data by selecting a specific date range. The date filter is set to *Last 28 days* by default.

To change the date range, click on the filter and then click on the arrow in the upper right corner to pick a date range from the drop-down menu. You can also create a custom date range by clicking on the first option.

> [!NOTE]
> The date range filter cannot be removed, but you can change the default date range at any time.

## Settings

Depending on the chart type you have chosen, you will be able to make different changes in the settings. You can find more information about the different chart types in the data visualizations section [here](../../data-visualizations/overview.md).

When you are ready with the visualization editor, you can save the dashboard in **My Analytics** ⇒ **My Dashboards**, a specific workspace, or a project.

If you want to find more information about the data sources, you can head [here](../../datasources/overview.md).
