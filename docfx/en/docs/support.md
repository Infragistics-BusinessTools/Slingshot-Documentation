# Slingshot Support

Need help with Slingshot? Here's how to reach us.

## Open a Support Case

The fastest way to get help is to open a support case at [account.slingshotapp.io/support-cases](https://account.slingshotapp.io/support-cases). Sign in with your Slingshot account to create a new case, attach screenshots, and track the status of your existing cases. You'll receive updates by email as our team works on your case.

Support cases are reviewed by our team on business days (Monday–Friday).

## Send Feedback from the App

You can report a problem without leaving Slingshot:

1. Click/tap your profile picture in the upper-right corner.

2. Click/tap **Give Feedback**.

3. Describe the issue. You can attach screenshots — with annotation tools to point at the problem — and include your system information, which helps our team diagnose faster.

See [Feedback](feedback.md) for more details.

## Browse the Knowledge Base

The [Slingshot Help Center](welcome-slingshot.md) has how-to guides for every feature — from [tasks](tasks.md) and [workspaces](workspaces.md) to analytics and integrations. It's the quickest way to answer "how do I…" questions on your own, any time.

## Stay in the Loop

Check the [Release Notes](release-notes.md) to see what's new, improved, and fixed in every Slingshot release.
