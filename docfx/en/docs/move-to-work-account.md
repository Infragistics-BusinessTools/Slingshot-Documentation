# Move to Your Work Account

If you started Slingshot with a personal email and your company now has a Slingshot account, you can send your workspaces and task lists to your work account. Your lists and tasks arrive ready to use, so you don't have to rebuild them.

Moving is a copy. Everything in your personal account stays exactly as it was.

## What Moves

| Moves | Stays behind |
|---|---|
| Task lists and their tasks, with their original dates | Dashboards |
| Task types | Assignees |
| Custom fields | Comments |
| | Attachments |
| | Automations |

## Before You Start

- You need to be the **owner** of each workspace you send.
- You need access to both accounts: the personal one you're sending from, and the work account you're sending to.
- Only workspaces in a personal account can be moved. A workspace that already belongs to a company account can't be.

## Sending Workspaces and Task Lists

1. In your personal account, open the overflow menu next to a workspace name and choose **Move to my work account**.
2. Check the workspaces and task lists you want to send. The workspace you started from is already checked.
3. Enter your **Work email**.
4. Click/tap on **Send link**.

Slingshot emails a link to your work address. The link expires in **7 days**.

> [!NOTE]
> The copy is taken when you send the link. Changes you make in your personal account afterward aren't included. To include them, cancel the transfer and send it again.

## Accepting in Your Work Account

1. Open the email at your work address and click/tap on the link.
2. Sign in to Slingshot with your work email. If you don't have an account yet, sign up with your work email.
3. Review what's being sent, then click/tap on **Accept transfer**.

Workspaces are added to your company's account. Task lists are added to **My Tasks**.

## Checking the Status of a Transfer

In your personal account, open the workspace's **Settings** and go to the **Transfers** tab. Each transfer shows one of these statuses:

| Status | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for acceptance** | **Resend link** if the email got lost, or **Cancel transfer**. |
| **Expired** | **Send again** to start a new transfer. |
| **Accepted** | Nothing more to do. The card shows who accepted it and when. |

## Troubleshooting

- **The link expired.** Links last 7 days. Go to the workspace's **Transfers** tab and click/tap on **Send again**.
- **"This workspace belongs to a company account and can't be moved."** The workspace is already part of a company account, so there's nothing to move.
- **The link doesn't work when you open it.** Make sure you're signed in with the work email the link was sent to.

For anything else, reach out to us through the in-app feedback option. See [Feedback](feedback.md).
